Register your school

One school account lets you create game accounts for your students and follow how far they have played. We verify every school admin first.

With a school account you can

  • person_add
    Register your students

    Enter each student's full name and Gmail. We email them a link to set their own password, so you never handle it.

  • groups
    Organize students into sections

    Create a section for each class, set a student limit, and choose the section when you register a student.

  • badge
    Add teachers

    Add teachers by Gmail and assign each one a section. They only see the students in their own section.

  • monitoring
    See story progress and badges

    Check which chapters each student finished and which badges they unlocked.

We check your document first. Once approved, you pay the one-time fee and your account is activated. We also email a verification link; open it before you log in.
