About the Game

WHAT IS LARO TAYO?

Laro Tayo is a thesis project that develops an educational game digitally adapting traditional Filipino street games for children and students. Designed for mobile devices, it brings classic games such as Sipa, Luksong Baka, Patintero, and Palosebo to life on the screen, so that players can enjoy them anytime and anywhere.

Many of these games are slowly being forgotten as children spend more time on modern gadgets. We created Laro Tayo to keep our traditional street games alive and to give the new generation a fun and exciting way to discover them. Along the way, players learn the history and culture behind each game, play with their friends in multiplayer, and take part in a playful experience that celebrates what it means to grow up Filipino.
